Cat Facts:

1. Cats sleep for 70% of their lives.
2. A group of cats is called a clowder.
3. Cats have 230 bones, while humans only have 206.
4. A cat’s nose print is unique, much like a human fingerprint.
5. Cats can rotate their ears 180 degrees.
6. The oldest known pet cat existed 9,500 years ago.
7. Cats can jump up to six times their length in one leap.
8. A cat’s whiskers are roughly as wide as its body to help measure gaps.
9. Unlike humans, cats can make over 100 different sounds.
10. The world’s oldest cat lived to be 38 years old.
